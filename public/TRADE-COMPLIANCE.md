## 3 · Incoterms® 2020 (P1)

#### 3.1 Default term

#### 3.2 Other available terms (on request)

- **FOB Guangzhou / Shenzhen** (Free On Board, named port of loading = Guangzhou or Shenzhen, China)
- Risk transfers when goods cross the ship's rail at the named port of loading
- Supplier arranges export clearance; buyer arranges import clearance, ocean freight, insurance from FOB point onward

- **EXW** (Ex Works) — buyer collects at our Heyuan factory
- **FCA** (Free Carrier) — buyer nominates carrier at named place
- **CIF** (Cost, Insurance & Freight) — supplier pays ocean freight + 110% insurance to named port
- **CFR** (Cost and Freight) — same as CIF but buyer insures
- **DAP** (Delivered At Place) — supplier delivers to buyer's named address, duties unpaid
- **DDP** (Delivered Duty Paid) — supplier pays all duties and delivers to buyer's door
- **DPU** (Delivered at Place Unloaded) — supplier delivers and unloads at named place

All terms per **ICC Publication 723E — Incoterms® 2020**. Quote must specify which term applies.

## 12 · Shipment & Logistics

#### 12.1 When you get a tracking number

- **Order flow:** 35% prepaid by the buyer when the order is placed → production completed → the logistics order number is issued and confirmed with the buyer → the balance is collected → the goods ship.
- **What the number is:** Bill of lading (B/L) for sea, air waybill (AWB) for air and courier
- **When it is issued:** Issued when production is complete, and confirmed with the buyer before the balance is settled — so you have the tracking number before the goods move. On sea the B/L is the title document; on air and courier it is the AWB or courier tracking number.
- **The one earlier case:** Samples are dispatched with a courier tracking number within 48 hours.
- **What you can track:** Sea: the B/L number is your container number and can be tracked through the forwarder. Air and courier: the AWB or courier number is trackable from the first scan.
- **Before shipment:** Stock catalogue formulas: 7–15 days after payment confirmation. Private label: 25–40 days after sample approval.

#### 12.2 What travels with the goods

- Commercial invoice (with HS code)
- Packing list
- Bill of lading (B/L) or air waybill (AWB)
- Certificate of origin (COO) China
- MSDS
- INCI disclosure
- COA for every production batch

#### 12.3 Channels and transit time

| Channel | Transit time | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sea freight — FOB Guangzhou / Shenzhen | 25–35 days to Europe and the US West Coast; 35–45 days to the US East Coast | The default lane for bulk palletised orders. Cheapest per unit, slowest to arrive. |
| Sea freight — DDP to your warehouse | 40–50 days door-to-door | We handle export customs, ocean freight, import clearance and the final mile. Duty and tax are inside the quote. Customs clearance and duty handled by us. |
| Air freight | 5–7 days door-to-door | For launches and restocks where the shelf date matters more than the freight bill. |
| Express courier — DHL / FedEx / UPS | 3–5 days | Samples and urgent top-ups. Freight is paid by the buyer. |

#### 12.4 Incoterms and freight

- **Default term:** FOB Guangzhou / Shenzhen (ICC Publication 723E). Risk transfers at the named port of loading. Any other term is quoted on request.
- **Also quoted:** EXW · FCA · CIF · CFR · DAP · DDP · DPU
- **How freight is charged:** Quoted per lane against your destination, the volume and the Incoterms term, and shown separately from the product price so you can compare suppliers on the same basis.
- **Samples:** Sample freight is paid by the buyer: USD 30–80 via DHL / FedEx / UPS depending on weight and destination.
- **DDP lanes:** On a DDP lane the freight, import duty and tax are one quote — no separate customs invoice to settle.
